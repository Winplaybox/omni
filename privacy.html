Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Omni AI Workflows (omni.winplaybox.in)

At Omni AI Workflows (omni.winplaybox.in), protecting your privacy is our foundational commitment. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices, zero-storage policy for sensitive credentials, and your rights under GDPR and CCPA.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect (Zero Credential Retention)

Omni does NOT store or log your private third-party API keys (e.g. OpenAI keys, Stripe secret keys, Slack webhook secrets, or AWS credentials) on our database servers. All workflow syntheses and canvas simulations are processed client-side or in stateless ephemeral compute instances.

2. Technical Log Data We Collect

Like standard web infrastructure, our hosting environment logs minimal diagnostics:

  • Client IP addresses and browser user-agent signatures
  • Date and time of requests
  • Referring URL and requested platform pages

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize browser localStorage solely to save your active workflow canvas state locally on your machine so you do not lose your diagram when refreshing the page. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies.

4. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you retain the right to access, rectify, or erase your data. Because we do not harvest personal profiles or sell consumer data, your digital privacy footprint on Omni remains pristine.

5. Contact Data Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to make an inquiry, please contact our team at privacy@winplaybox.in or through the WinPlayBox Contact Center.

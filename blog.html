Autonomous Multi-Channel Content Repurposing Pipeline (YouTube to 5 Tweets, LinkedIn & Newsletter)

Creating high-converting social media distribution from long-form video or audio recordings is typically one of the most time-consuming operations for modern creator businesses. This autonomous pipeline completely removes human latency: as soon as a video renders on YouTube, the audio is ingested, transcribed, transformed into platform-specific native formats, and scheduled across three platforms.

1. Pipeline Architecture & Data Flow

[YouTube RSS Trigger] ──> [Whisper Large Audio Transcript] ──> [Gemini 1.5 Pro Context Hooks] ──> [Canva / Remotion Carousel Builder] ──> [Buffer / Ghost CMS Multi-Dispatch]

2. Key Node Operations

  • Node 1 (Trigger): Subscribes to YouTube PubSubHubbub webhooks. Extracts video ID and initiates stream download.
  • Node 2 (Transcription): Transcribes raw audio into speaker-diarized text using OpenAI Whisper Large v3 with punctuation restoration.
  • Node 3 (LLM Reasoning): Passes full transcript to Google Gemini 1.5 Pro with a system prompt optimized for Twitter thread cadence, LinkedIn hooks, and newsletter body text.
  • Node 4 (Asset Rendering): Formats extracted takeaway points into a 5-slide visual carousel via Remotion.
  • Node 5 (Distribution): Queues the thread to Buffer, posts to LinkedIn API, and saves a draft article on Ghost CMS.
